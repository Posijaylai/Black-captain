About the Game

"妈妈来了！快关电脑" is an exciting, fast-paced game where you play as a student secretly using the computer. Your mission is to close all open windows before your mom reaches your room. The tension rises as mom's progress bar fills up - you have only 10 seconds when she appears!

How to Play

  • check_circle Use A / D or arrow keys to move left/right
  • check_circle Click the × button to close windows
  • check_circle Close ALL windows before mom reaches 100% progress
  • check_circle When mom appears, you have only 10 seconds!
  • check_circle Each closed window gives 10 points, bonus for completing

Pro Tip

Don't let her see your screen! In real life, use the Alt+Tab shortcut if you hear footsteps! But in this game, you need to actually close those windows before time runs out. Focus on windows closest to you first!
